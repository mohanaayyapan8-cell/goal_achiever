How streaks work

  • A day counts when all its goals are done and you submitted both assessments (written and speaking) that day, in your local time ({{ request.user.profile.timezone }}).
  • Each completed day adds 1 to your streak.
  • Missing one day interrupts your streak (a warning shows), but you keep it. Complete the next day to continue.
  • Missing two days in a row resets your current streak to 0. Your history, badges and longest streak are never deleted.
